How the replay works

Two berths serve a single vessel type each. A flexible berth serves both. At each arrival or relevant berth release, the scheduler assigns vessels that have already arrived. It fills dedicated berths first. Service runs to completion.

The shortest-service policy ranks vessels by estimated handling time. The third policy gives vessels already waiting 12 hours priority at the next compatible assignment. Arrival disruptions delay a group of vessels to a shared arrival time. All scenarios use synthetic inputs, and the results apply to those assumptions.
